# Knowledge policy

Knowledge is durable only when it is reusable, evidence-linked, scoped, and
safe to share in this repository.

## Admission gate

A knowledge change should state:

- the recurring question or failure it addresses;
- the evidence and environment where it was observed;
- the boundary within which the guidance is valid;
- a concrete retrieval phrase or situation;
- what would invalidate or retire it.

Do not store credentials, private prompts, raw conversation logs, absolute user
paths, transient command output, or unreviewed guesses. Prefer a short durable
rule over a dump of execution history. When evidence changes, amend or retire
the knowledge through a bounded Issue and pull request.
